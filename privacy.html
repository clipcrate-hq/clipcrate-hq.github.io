ClipCrate_HQ Uploader – Privacy Policy

ClipCrate_HQ Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the ClipCrate_HQ YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and publish times on the owner's own channel.

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person. The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer and is used only to upload to the owner's channel. You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
