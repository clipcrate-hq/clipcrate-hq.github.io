ClipCrate_HQ Uploader – Privacy Policy

ClipCrate_HQ Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the ClipCrate_HQ YouTube channel, The Clip Crate Facebook Page and the clipcrate_hq Instagram account. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and publish times on the owner's own channel. It also uses Meta's Graph API (including the app "ClipCrate_HQ Poster") to publish Reels and their captions to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account.

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person. The Google and Meta access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to publish to the owner's own accounts. You can revoke Google access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and Meta access in your Facebook settings under Business Integrations.

Data deletion: this tool holds no personal data about other people, so there is nothing to delete for them. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email the contact address below.

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.
